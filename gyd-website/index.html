12-STEP PhD JOURNEY / TIMELINE

1

Mark Sheet Verification

Send your updated resume with 10th standard marksheet, 12th standard marksheets, MPhil. certificate, semester marksheets, consolidated marksheets, degree certificates for certificate verification and validation of your eligibility for PhD registration.

2

Supervisor Meeting

Visiting the PhD research supervisor in this college or university as per our instruction and guidance (research supervisor will be allotted by our organisation).

3

Certificate Submission in College

Research proposal presentation by you in the PhD admitted college and also complete the certificate verification process and certificate submission process in the college on the same day.

4

Issue of PhD Registration Certificate & PhD ID Card

Once Certificate Verification is Completion, and PhD Course fee is paid, PhD Registration Letter and PhD Course ID Card will be issued.

5

PhD Thesis And Research Paper Preparation

Learn, Get Trained, & Co-ordinate with our company to successfully complete 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th & 5th Chapter of your thesis and publications.

6

Model & Real Draft of your Speech & DC Meeting & Viva-Voce Exam

Get written Speech draft of your presentation for around 30-35 minutes from our office, which you can use during your pre-meeting & Viva-Voce Exam.

7

Attending Doctoral Committee meetings

Attend 1st/2nd/3rd doctoral committee meeting in front of the HOD and your supervisor and explain your research topic for 30 minutes during 1st, 2nd, 3rd year of your PhD degree course.

8

Synopsis Submission at University

After completing 2 Doctoral committee meetings, submit your synopsis in your phd admitted college.

9

Thesis Submission at University

Exactly after 3 months from the date of your synopsis submission, you will be eligible to submit your final thesis in the university.

10

Final Viva Voce Presentation

Based on the thesis submitted by you, you have to make a 45 minutes presentation as final viva voce exam in your college.

11

Get Provisional Certificate from College

After 3 months of your viva, you will receive your provisional certificate for your phd degree.

12

Get PhD Degree Certificate from College / University in Convocation Ceremony

After 1 year from the date of your viva, you will receive your phd degree certificate in the college or university convocation ceremony.
